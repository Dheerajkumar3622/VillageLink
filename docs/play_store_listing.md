## Full Description (4000 chars max)

🚌 **VillageLink - India's First Rural Super App** 🚌

Connecting villages with modern services. VillageLink brings together transport, food, and agriculture in one powerful app designed specifically for rural India.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🚌 **YATRALINK - TRANSPORT**
• Book buses, autos, and shared rides
• Real-time GPS tracking of your vehicle
• Digital tickets with QR code verification
• Daily passes for regular commuters
• Track your driver's location live

🍽️ **FOODLINK - FOOD SERVICES**
• Order from local mess and restaurants
• Monthly meal subscription plans
• Kitchen/mess management for owners
• Track your food delivery live

🌾 **GRAMMANDI - FRESH PRODUCE**
• Buy vegetables, fruits directly from farmers
• Dairy products - milk, curd, ghee
• Wholesale bidding for vendors
• Cold storage booking facility
• Real-time mandi prices

📦 **PARCELLINK - DELIVERIES**
• Send packages village to village
• Track your parcel in real-time
• Safe and secure delivery

💼 **ROJGARLINK - JOBS**
• Find local employment opportunities
• Skill-based job matching
• Daily wage work listings

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🔥 **KEY FEATURES:**

✅ Works offline - cached for low network areas
✅ Hindi & English language support
✅ Voice search for easy navigation
✅ Secure payments via UPI/Cards
✅ SOS emergency button
✅ Guardian mode for family safety

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

👨‍🌾 **FOR FARMERS (KisanApp)**

Access <https://villagelink.app/kisan>

• List your produce for sale
• Track milk collection and payments
• Get best prices through bidding

🚗 **FOR DRIVERS (DriverApp)**

Access <https://villagelink.app/driver>

• Accept ride requests
• Track your earnings
• Manage your schedule

🏪 **FOR VENDORS (VyapariApp)**

Access <https://villagelink.app/vyapari>

• Bid on wholesale produce
• Manage inventory
• Track orders
